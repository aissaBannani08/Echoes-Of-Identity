Privacy & consent

Read our privacy and consent information +−

If you create a member account, the site processes your account email and profile information through its Supabase integration. Signed-in members can upload a profile avatar to Supabase Storage. People with access to the project’s Supabase account may access information stored there.

The stories in this archive are public. We use a contributor’s name, preferred name, or no public name according to the attribution agreed for that story. Text, recordings, or photographs should be published only with the contributor’s consent for that use.

This website currently has no built-in story submission form. Please contact the project before sending a testimony, recording, or photograph. The “Join the Project” application opens a Google Form; information entered there is handled by that service.

To ask about a story, request a correction, discuss anonymity, or request that a story be withdrawn, email info@echoesofidentity.org.
