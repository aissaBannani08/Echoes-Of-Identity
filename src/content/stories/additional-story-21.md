<p>This manuscript was assembled only from the user's previously supplied interview notes, testimony drafts, transcriptions, and research-writing files. No outside sources were consulted or added. Existing first-person testimony is retained in first person where a draft was already available. Where the source material consists of research notes summarizing interviews rather than a full transcript, the piece is written as a third-person oral-history narrative and is not presented as a verbatim quotation or as a participant's exact words.</p><p>The earlier planning discussion estimated a theoretical ceiling of roughly 75–80 story candidates across a larger body of interviews. The files recoverable here do not contain all of that underlying material: in particular, the complete Daniel Cohen interview transcript and several of the Palestinian interview records discussed in earlier conversations are not present. To avoid inventing testimony or disguising repeated material as separate stories, this document includes only distinct narratives that can be supported by the accessible sources. It is a recovered working collection, not a claim that all 75 planned stories have been reconstructed.</p><p>Editorial treatment follows the agreed method: preserve the interviewee's meaning; improve clarity and flow; separate genuinely distinct experiences or themes; do not add events, dialogue, motives, dates, or biographical details that the source does not establish. Any uncertainty or incomplete transcription should be checked against the original recording before publication.</p>
